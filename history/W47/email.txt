Monday view — week of 16–22 Nov 2026

Anthropic listed at a $1.3T market cap, and Europe's early-stage money went to AI tooling for chips, robots and space.

Each ● is one source family (Sifted, TLDR, Term Sheet, Techmeme, etc.) carrying the item. ●●●●● means 5 or more. Breadth of coverage, not importance: a press release reprinted by three deal digests gets three dots.

1. The week's story: Anthropic goes public ●●●●● (9 families)

- Wed 18 Nov: priced at $83 a share, above the range. About $81.3bn raised, the largest IPO on record.
- Thu 19 Nov: opened at $111.50 (+34%), closed at $97.17 (+17%). Market cap about $1.3T.
- Fri 20 Nov: AI sell-off. Asian tech fell: SoftBank about −15%, TSMC −5%+, Samsung and SK Hynix −6%+. The greenshoe took total proceeds to $93.5bn.

For us: public investors have now priced a frontier lab. Late-stage AI marks get a public comparable, and early-stage pricing does not.

5. Market move

OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra: API tokens 20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra, at $8 / $40 per million input/output tokens (Techmeme, AlphaSignal, 18 Nov). OpenAI also claims under one-third of GPT-6 Astra's cost per task on its own evals. Google's Gemini 4 Neon: $3 / $15 per million input/output tokens. Inference got cheaper again, which helps application startups and squeezes resellers.
